手機版（iOS 及 Android）

手機版是離線遊戲，沒有帳戶、廣告、分析或追蹤。我們不收集、不接收，也不分享任何個人資料。

你的卡牌、牌組與進度保存在此裝置上的遊戲儲存空間；遊戲亦會在其檔案空間保留一份旅程副本，以免系統清理網頁儲存時遺失進度。如你使用 iCloud 備份、電腦備份或其他裝置備份，這些備份可能包括遊戲資料；備份由你按自己的設定透過 Apple 或其他服務處理，我們不會收到。你匯出旅程備份檔時，由你決定把檔案存到哪裡或傳給誰。刪除遊戲會刪除裝置上的遊戲資料，但不會刪除裝置備份或你已匯出的檔案。

網頁版帳戶（mythseal.com）

網頁版可選擇以電郵登入，用來備份旅程。只有你主動登入時，我們才會處理：你的電郵地址、一次性驗證碼、登入用的安全 cookie，以及你選擇上傳的旅程備份（卡牌、等級、歷練與對局紀錄）。資料由 Supabase（倫敦）儲存，驗證碼電郵經 Resend 寄出，網站由 Cloudflare 提供。你可隨時在遊戲內刪除雲端備份；如要刪除帳戶，請按下方聯絡方式聯絡我們。

The iOS and Android apps

The mobile apps are offline games with no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. We do not collect, receive or share any personal data.

Your cards, decks and progress are kept in the game's storage on your device, and the game also keeps a copy of your journey in its own files so that progress is not lost if the system clears web storage. If you use iCloud Backup, a computer backup or another device backup, those backups may include the game's data; they are handled by Apple or the backup service under your own settings, and we never receive them. When you export a journey file, you decide where it is saved or who it is sent to. Deleting the app deletes the game's data from the device, but not from device backups or files you have exported.

Website accounts (mythseal.com)

On the website you can optionally sign in with your email to back up your journey. Only when you choose to sign in do we process: your email address, one-time sign-in codes, a secure session cookie, and the journey backup you choose to upload (cards, levels, experience and battle history). Data is stored with Supabase (London), sign-in emails are sent through Resend, and the site is served by Cloudflare. You can delete your cloud backup in the game at any time; to delete your account, contact us using the details below.
