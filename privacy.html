私隱政策 · Privacy policy

萬象契 Mythseal · 生效日期 Effective 2026-10-12

手機版（iOS 及 Android）

手機版是離線遊戲。我們不收集、不傳送、也不分享任何個人資料。你的卡牌、牌組與進度只儲存在你的裝置上；刪除遊戲便會一併刪除這些資料。手機版沒有帳戶、廣告、分析或追蹤。

網頁版帳戶（mythseal.com）

網頁版可選擇以電郵登入，用來備份旅程。只有你主動登入時，我們才會處理：你的電郵地址、一次性驗證碼、登入用的安全 cookie，以及你選擇上傳的旅程備份（卡牌、等級、歷練與對局紀錄）。資料由 Supabase（倫敦）儲存，驗證碼電郵經 Resend 寄出，網站由 Cloudflare 提供。你可隨時在遊戲內刪除雲端備份；如要刪除帳戶，請電郵聯絡我們。

The iOS and Android apps

The mobile apps are offline games. We do not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Your cards, decks and progress are stored only on your device, and deleting the app deletes them. The apps have no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Website accounts (mythseal.com)

On the website you can optionally sign in with your email to back up your journey. Only when you choose to sign in do we process: your email address, one-time sign-in codes, a secure session cookie, and the journey backup you choose to upload (cards, levels, experience and battle history). Data is stored with Supabase (London), sign-in emails are sent through Resend, and the site is served by Cloudflare. You can delete your cloud backup in the game at any time; to delete your account, email us.
