# Name and domain workshop

Research snapshot: **2026-09-21**. Current private repository name remains `shanhai-duel`; the game has not been renamed. These are creative candidates, not cleared trademarks, registered domains or final release titles.

## Shortlist

| Priority | Chinese / English | Why it fits | Domain candidate and observed registry result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **萬象契 · Mythseal** | Short international name; a seal suggests a collectible with identity and history. 萬象 leaves room for Wukong, Three Kingdoms, literary figures and original characters together. | `mythseal.com` — registry returned HTTP 404, no registration record found at check time. |
| **2** | **山海契 · Shanhai Oath** | Closest to the current atmosphere. The oath is the bond between a player and a card that travels and grows. | `shanhaioath.com` — registry returned HTTP 404, no registration record found at check time. |
| **3** | **千古契 · Oathscroll** | Emphasizes figures stepping out of classical books and histories, with a scroll-like collectible identity. | `oathscroll.com` is registered. `playoathscroll.com` returned HTTP 404, no registration record found at check time. |

Creative preference: **萬象契 / Mythseal**. It is compact, flexible across the planned roster and easy to carry into an app icon, card seal and expansion name. Try saying and typing it with English-speaking players before selecting it; the “th” sound may be less comfortable for some audiences. This preference is design judgment, not evidence of market demand.

Possible copy to try:

- **萬象契 · Mythseal** — 一張卡，一段傳說。
- **山海契 · Shanhai Oath** — 與神話同行，讓歷練留名。
- **千古契 · Oathscroll** — 千古人物，落子成篇。

An app-store subtitle could describe the product plainly, such as “Mythic Card Duels” / “東方神話策略卡牌”. Final title/subtitle lengths and localization need checking in the target stores before release.

## Findings that change the naming decision

The current Chinese title **山海弈** already appears as the title of another developer's eight-player mythology auto-battler on [TapTap](https://www.taptap.cn/app/940623). Keep it as the internal working title while selecting a distinct release brand.

The broader Shanhai category is also crowded: [Shanhai Bestiary: Card Duel](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shanhai-bestiary-card-duel/id6805338907) is listed on the App Store, and [山海靈契](https://www.taptap.cn/app/937364?os=android) is another mythology collection title on TapTap. This favors a distinctive paired Chinese/English identity over relying on “Shanhai” alone. It does not establish a legal conflict for every Shanhai-based name.

Bounded searches used the exact English candidates, their compact spellings and Chinese candidates with game terms. They did not establish a clearly matching full game title for the three proposed Chinese/English pairs. These checks are incomplete: unindexed games, regional listings, domains, company names and marks may still overlap. “Oathscroll” also appears as descriptive language in tabletop/fantasy material, so the word itself should not be treated as newly coined.

HTTP 404 means the registry did not return a registration record. It is **not** a registrar quote, guaranteed availability, reservation or trademark clearance. A registered domain is not assumed to be for sale. Availability can change; no domain has been purchased, DNS configured or seller contacted.

## After choosing a direction

1. Check pronunciation, spelling, icon legibility and how the title sits on a card back in both languages.
2. Search the intended app stores, relevant trademark registries, company names and social handles for the exact final spelling and close variants.
3. Check registrar availability, first-year/renewal pricing and premium status immediately before a separately authorized purchase.
4. Register using the project's chosen account and recovery setup. Plan `play`, `cards` and `support` subdomains only after ownership is established.
5. Rename the visible game, package/store identifiers and repository together with redirects where appropriate. Existing saves should migrate across branding changes without being reset.
