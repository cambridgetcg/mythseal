# Playtest record · O7

**Status: `proposal · 2026-10-21` for the form; each session added under
[Sessions](#sessions) is a `record` of its own date.** This is packet O7 from
the [onboarding benchmarks](BENCHMARKS-HABBY-SUPERCELL.md#7-候選工作包proposal).
On 2026-10-21 the owner confirmed that onboarding is judged by human playtests
only, with no telemetry ([benchmarks §8, item 6](BENCHMARKS-HABBY-SUPERCELL.md#8-owner-決定)).
This page is where those playtests are written down: one form per tester.

## What it feeds

- **D3's exit.** First-battle teaching is finished when the owner has watched
  at least three first-time players, on fresh profiles and on a phone, and
  noted round-1 HP lost, whether they add pills before committing and whether
  they can explain a reveal (the Exit paragraph under
  [First battle teaching](BATTLE-LAYOUT-PLAN.md#first-battle-teaching--2026-10-12),
  and the D3 status in [ROADMAP M4](../ROADMAP.md#m4--grow-the-game-presentation-and-competitive-play)).
- **M4's acceptance.** A new player completes the battle → travel/daily →
  backpack/upgrade loop without help, can explain a reveal, can identify the
  exact copies that gained XP, and finds repeated play brisk (the Acceptance
  paragraph of [ROADMAP M4](../ROADMAP.md#m4--grow-the-game-presentation-and-competitive-play)).
- **Choices waiting on playtests.** The five destinations stay open unless
  newcomers get lost; if they do, the lobby moves to option (B), quiet until
  the first win ([benchmarks §8, item 2](BENCHMARKS-HABBY-SUPERCELL.md#8-owner-決定)).
  Time to first result also puts a real figure beside the benchmarks'
  unmeasured estimate of 4–5 minutes for the first battle
  ([§5](BENCHMARKS-HABBY-SUPERCELL.md#5-clash-royale-是最接近的參照但類比在哪裡斷開)).

A form with gaps still counts for the parts it has.

## Running a session

- **Who counts as new.** Someone who has never played Mythseal, on a fresh
  profile. An experienced tester is still worth watching; mark them "no" so
  they don't count toward D3.
- **Say little.** Hand over the phone without explaining the rules. Answer when
  asked and note that you did, because the M4 loop counts what a player does
  without help.
- **Time it yourself,** with a clock you hold, from the first screen to the
  first 勝負已定 page. The game measures nothing.
- **Ask at the end, not during:** "What happened in that round?" for the
  reveal, "Which card grew from that battle?" for 歷練, and "Would you play
  again?" in their words.
- **A second session** is the same tester coming back, later that day or on
  another day. Note it if you see it, otherwise write "not observed". Supercell's
  own lesson is that tests judged on day-7 retention did not predict
  long-term behaviour ([benchmarks §6](BENCHMARKS-HABBY-SUPERCELL.md#6-哪些適合-mythseal),
  the A/B row), which is why the form asks about coming back.

## What stays out, and why

- **Telemetry and recordings.** The game records none of this, and the privacy draft
  (`privacy.html`) says the mobile apps have no analytics or tracking. Watching
  a person shows where they hesitate, which a counter would not.
- **Personal data.** This repository is public, so anything written here is
  published. Use an alias: no name, contact details, age, photo, voice or
  screen recording. Device model, OS version and build number describe the
  build, not the tester.
