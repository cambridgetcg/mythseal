# 西行 · special-print art studies

Created 2026-09-22 with the built-in imagegen tool. These three original
1024×1536 portrait illustrations are consumed by the local `atelier.html`
preview. They are visual studies for WX-01, not issued or owned card copies.
No reference image was supplied to the generator. The existing classics atlas
was inspected to describe Wukong's simian face, circlet, bronze armour and sash.
No One Piece illustration is bundled with the project or used as a generator input.

The standard portrait remains the existing `assets/classics.png` tile at row 0,
column 1. New images are copied unchanged into the project; the original generated
files remain in the private local image archive. UI frames, names and rules are
rendered in HTML/CSS, never baked into the illustration.

| Printing | Project asset | Art direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 異畫 · 踏碎凌霄 | `assets/expansion/wukong-alt.png` | Dynamic cloud-gate scene, teal and crimson |
| 墨卷 · 大鬧天宮 | `assets/expansion/wukong-ink.png` | Original paper-and-ink narrative vignettes |
| 天工 · 定海神珍 | `assets/expansion/wukong-treasure.png` | Dark sea palace, bronze, gold and jade |

These are invented compositions based on classical motifs, not replicas of
historical artwork or adaptations of modern film/game costumes. The ink page
combines different episodes as a visual montage; it is not a claim that the
events occurred simultaneously. Colour and material styling do not change rules.
